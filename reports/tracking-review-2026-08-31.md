## Fibre

**Worth tracking, with a practical target of 30g/day.** UK adult guidance does not calculate fibre from height or bodyweight, so the stored 193cm and 114.8kg do not require a different starting number. Fibre supports bowel health, and higher-fibre foods can help make meals more filling. Choose a variety: wholegrain bread or oats, beans/lentils, vegetables and whole fruit. Substitute these within existing meals rather than simply adding calories. [NHS fibre guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/digestive-health/how-to-get-more-fibre-into-your-diet/).

Increase gradually and drink enough fluid as intake rises. [NIDDK guidance](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/constipation/eating-diet-nutrition).
